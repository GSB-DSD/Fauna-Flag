A BECHDEL TEST FOR THE ANIMALS

Would you call it safe for the animals?

You'll be shown films one at a time, each with a short dossier on how its animals were portrayed and treated. Give each one a red, amber or green flag. At the end you'll get a percentile breakdown of your own line on cruelty, and see how it compares with the curated archive and with everyone else who has taken the test.

RED

Real harm, cruelty, or exploitation of animals. Unacceptable.

AMBER

A grey area: distressing themes, simulated peril, or questionable handling.

GREEN

Safe: humane, supervised, CGI or animated, with no distress to animals.
